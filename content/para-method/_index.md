## The PARA Method

The PARA Method is a simple, intuitive system for organizing your digital information. It is based on the idea that you should organize your files by their purpose, rather than by their topic.

### Key Benefits

- **Action-Oriented**: Helps you focus on what you need to do, rather than on what you have.
- **Flexible**: Can be used to organize any type of information, from your personal files to your work projects.
- **Simplicity**: Easy to learn and use, even if you have never used a filing system before.

### Who Should Use This System?

This system is ideal for:
- **Creatives & Entrepreneurs**: Who juggle many active projects at once.
- **Students**: Managing different classes (Projects) and ongoing study topics (Areas).
- **Knowledge Workers**: Who need to separate "active work" from "reference material."
- **Productivity Enthusiasts**: Who want their file system to match their to-do list.

### How It Works: A Practical Example

The system organizes everything into four top-level categories based on actionability.

**Structure:**
- `1. Projects` (Active goals with a deadline)
    - `Website Redesign`
- `2. Areas` (Ongoing responsibilities without a deadline)
    - `Finances`
    - `Health`
- `3. Resources` (Topics of interest)
    - `Web Design Inspiration`
- `4. Archives` (Completed projects/Inactive items)
    - `2023 Website Project`

**Example Scenario:**
You are working on redesigning your personal website.

1.  This is an active goal, so it goes in **Projects**.
2.  Navigate to `1. Projects/Website Redesign/`.
3.  Save your new logo draft as `logo_v1.png`.

Once the website is launched (project complete), you move the folder to **4. Archives**. The final logo asset you keep for future use might get moved to **3. Resources/Brand Assets**.

### How to Use

1. **Download** the zip file above.
2. **Extract** the contents to a folder on your computer.
3. **Run the script**:
   - **Windows**: Double-click the `create_para_method.bat` file.
   - **macOS / Linux**: Open a terminal, navigate to the extracted folder, make the script executable with `chmod +x create_para_method.sh`, and run it with `./create_para_method.sh`.
